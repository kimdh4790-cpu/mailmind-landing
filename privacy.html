Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

MailMind AI is an AI email assistant available as a mobile app (Android and iOS) and a Chrome extension. It helps you write, translate and summarize email replies. This policy explains what data we access, where it goes, how long we keep it, and your choices.

1. Mail accounts you can connect

  • Gmail (Google sign-in) and Outlook (Microsoft sign-in)
  • iCloud Mail, Yahoo Mail, Yahoo Japan Mail and Naver Mail, using an app password you create with that provider

App passwords for these mail accounts are stored only on your device. We do not store them on our servers.

2. Information we access

  • Email content you open or ask us to reply to, translate or summarize.
  • Inbox preview for the AI Brief. When you open the home screen of the app, the sender, subject and a text preview of your most recent messages (up to 30, across the accounts you connected) are sent to our server to produce the AI Brief (a short summary and priority list). This happens automatically when the home screen loads, not only when you tap a button. The results are cached for up to 24 hours.
  • Attachments (for example PDF files and images) only when you ask us to analyze them. Compressed (.zip) attachments you open in the app are unpacked on your device; their contents are not sent to our server.
  • Account information: your sign-in email address, display name, plan and usage counts.
  • Profile you enter: name, job title, company, signature, business card text and saved phrases. In the app these are stored on your device and sent with a request only when needed to write the reply. In the Chrome extension they are kept in Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome synchronizes across your signed-in browsers through your Google account.
  • Chrome extension status check. When a supported webmail page loads, the extension asks our server for your plan and usage counters, sending only your account email address. Email content is sent only when you click a button for an AI feature, and the extension does not send your inbox list.
  • Document profile (optional): company details and tax registration numbers you enter to generate business documents. These are stored on our server so the feature works across devices.
  • Learned style: your role, industry and preferred tone, and for people you write to, their name, industry, tone and relationship, used to adjust future replies.
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) if you enable notifications.

3. Google API scopes we request

The Chrome extension requests only openid, email and profile to sign you in; it reads the message you have open on the webmail page itself and does not use the Gmail API. The mobile app requests the additional Gmail scopes below.

  • openid, email, profile: to sign you in and identify your account.
  • gmail.modify: to read the messages and attachments you work with, to mark a message as read, and to move a message to Trash when you choose to delete it in the app.
  • gmail.send: to send a reply or new message only after you tap send in the app.

We do not permanently delete your emails, and we do not use your mailbox for any purpose other than the features described here.

4. How your data is processed

When you request an AI feature, the relevant email text (and attachment text if you asked for it) is sent over HTTPS to our API server, which forwards it to an AI provider to generate the result and returns it to your device.

  • Our API runs on Cloudflare Workers. When you save a generated business document as PDF, the document content is sent over HTTPS (through a Cloudflare Tunnel) to a conversion server we operate on Oracle Cloud. It converts the document in memory and returns the PDF to you; it does not save the document, and it blocks the page being converted from loading outside resources.
  • AI providers: OpenAI (primary) and Anthropic (fallback). They process the text we send under their own terms; see the OpenAI Privacy Policy and the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • We do not use your email content to train our own models, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

6. Who we share data with

  • AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic): email content needed to generate results, as described above.
  • Cloudflare: hosting of our API, data storage, and the secure tunnel to our conversion server.
  • Oracle Cloud: a server we operate for processing and PDF conversion.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): push notifications.
  • Paddle: payment processing for paid plans on our website. Paddle receives your billing details and email; we receive the plan and subscription status.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Security

  • Data sent between the app or extension and our API uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Mail account app passwords stay on your device.
  • Access to our systems is limited to what is needed to run the service.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

MailMind AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Gmail data only to provide and improve the user-facing email features described here. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not allow humans to read it unless you give consent for support, it is required for security or abuse investigation, or the law requires it.

9. Your choices and deletion

  • Revoke MailMind AI's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Disconnect a mail account or sign out in the app to remove its credentials from your device. Uninstalling the app or removing the extension stops all access.
  • To access or delete your server-side data, email hello@mailmind.yongcha.app with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We delete your account data within 30 days of a verified request. This removes your account, subscription, usage, saved phrases, team, push token, document profile and learned style records. Two things cannot be linked to you and are handled differently: translation and mail-summary caches are keyed by a content fingerprint, so they simply expire (up to 7 days and 24 hours), and payment records held by Paddle must be requested from Paddle.

10. Children

MailMind AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when we change how we handle data. Questions: hello@mailmind.yongcha.app.
