FAQ

Common questions

How do I start? Do I need a Google account? +
Add the Chrome extension and open any email in Gmail, Outlook, Naver, iCloud, Yahoo or Yahoo Japan — the MailMind panel appears next to it. The extension signs you in with the Google account in your Chrome. The mobile apps for Android and iPhone (coming soon) also let you sign up with just your email address and a 6-digit code, or with Apple on iPhone, so no Google account is needed there. Signing in only creates your MailMind account; you connect your mailboxes separately.
Does MailMind read all my emails? +
No. In the Chrome extension, MailMind reads only the email you have open, and only when you click an AI button. In the mobile app, the home screen AI Brief sends the sender, subject and a short preview of your most recent messages (up to 30) to produce a summary; full email content is sent only when you use an AI feature. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
Which languages are supported? +
MailMind supports 17 languages including Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Italian, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, and Hindi. Any combination works — Japanese to Portuguese, Arabic to Korean, anything.
What if I don't speak the language at all? +
That's exactly what MailMind is for. The Smart Reply feature reads the email, shows you a summary in your language, then writes a perfect reply in the sender's language. You never need to understand the original email.
How does document analysis work? +
Open an email with an attachment and tap AI Analysis. MailMind reads PDF, Word, Excel/CSV, PowerPoint, image, HWP and OpenDocument files and shows the key facts — amounts, dates, deadlines and obligations — in your language, then uses them when you generate the reply. Analysis runs only when you tap it, never automatically. ZIP archives and scanned PDFs without text can't be read yet; extract the files or attach a text PDF instead. Available on Pro (50 / month), Pro+ (150 / month).
What documents can MailMind create? +
Quotes, invoices, purchase orders, contracts, NDAs, proposals, reports, cover letters, formal requests and greeting cards — over 1,200 document types across 26 countries, in any of 17 languages. Business documents come with calculated totals, tables and charts, and download as PDF, Word, Excel or HTML. Document generation is included in Pro+ (50 / month).
Are generated contracts legally valid? +
They are AI drafts, not legal advice. MailMind uses each country's common layout and wording and only fills in details that appear in the email, but you should review every document — and have contracts checked by a professional — before signing or sending. Official tax invoices that must be issued through a government system (for example Brazil NF-e or Mexico CFDI) are marked as drafts.
What's the difference between Smart Reply and Compare Replies? +
Compare Replies writes 2 versions (Basic) or 3 versions (Pro and above) in different styles so you can pick one. Smart Reply (Pro and above) also gives you a summary of the email in your language first, so you can check what was asked before choosing a reply in the sender's language.
What AI model does it use? +
MailMind uses OpenAI models as its primary AI and Anthropic’s Claude as a backup, combined with our own language, culture and tone rules so replies feel natural and culturally appropriate.
Is my email data safe? +
Yes. Email text and attachments are not kept on our servers after your request is processed (only short-lived caches of results, such as translations for up to 7 days). We never sell your emails or use them for advertising or to train AI models.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes, cancel anytime. No hidden fees, no lock-in — you keep your paid features until the end of the billing period you already paid for.
